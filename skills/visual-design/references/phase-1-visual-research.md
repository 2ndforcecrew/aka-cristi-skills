## Sources and intended use
- Awwwards (https://www.awwwards.com/): research experimental websites, editorial layouts, interactive storytelling, typography, motion and usability. The Awwwards *Hot Right Now* 2023 publication warns against copying trends without brand strategy: https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/gallery/2023/07/HOT-RIGHT-NOW-BOOK-2023.pdf
- Pinterest (https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=cool%20graphic%20design): discovery pool for graphic design, fashion posters, art direction, Swiss/experimental typography. Pinterest's 2026 trends and palettes are context, not mandatory brand colors: https://business.pinterest.com/pdf/pinterest-predicts/2026-marketing-playbook/ and https://newsroom.pinterest.com/pdf/pinterest-palette-2026-pdf/
- User seed keyword: **cool website**. Expand with **experimental editorial website**, **fashion portfolio web design**, **creative agency typography**, **art direction poster**, **Swiss typography**, **experimental graphic design**.

## Style DNA extraction — for each selected reference
Record source URL, creator (when known), date accessed, screenshot or asset permission, and 1–5 observations for:
1. Hierarchy and focal point
2. Grid, crop and whitespace
3. Typography character and scale
4. Photography and color grading
5. Motion/interaction (web only)
6. Mobile layout and reduced-motion fallback
7. Originality versus recognizable trend imitation
8. Brand and conversion purpose

## Art direction constraints
- Preserve the strongest cinematic/fashion-editorial tension of first-round concepts.
- User rejected second-round outcome as inferior; **Less Is More** means remove visual noise, not flatten personality.
- Do not invent or alter the canonical AKA.CRISTI A logo; use the supplied source asset when available, otherwise a text-only placeholder.
- References are for analysis, **not** for direct copying, scraping copyrighted assets, or representing third-party work as original.
- Default palette: black/white/silver/gray, unless the brief or reference evidence supports a deliberate exception.
- Website prototypes must remain responsive, accessible, performant, and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
