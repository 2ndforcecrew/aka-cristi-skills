# luxury-minimalism
Status: candidate knowledge; observed reference, not field validated.
## Visual grammar and execution
Off-white and black fashion page with huge wordmark behind walking model, black editorial strip, three evenly spaced collection images and portrait-led closing section. Use negative space, restrained typography and tactile photography; separate editorial and shopping actions; preserve wordmark legibility.
## Selection
Use when the brief benefits from this visual language; adapt palette, subject and typography to brand and audience rather than copying the reference.
