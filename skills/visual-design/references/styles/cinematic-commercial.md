# cinematic-commercial
Status: candidate knowledge; observed reference, not field validated.
## Visual grammar and execution
Orange-red portrait silhouette, large white left-aligned headline, dark trust strip, monochrome three-card portfolio. Use warm directional light as the hero focal point, then switch to neutral project imagery; preserve CTA contrast and avoid unverifiable client logos.
## Selection
Use when the brief benefits from this visual language; adapt palette, subject and typography to brand and audience rather than copying the reference.
