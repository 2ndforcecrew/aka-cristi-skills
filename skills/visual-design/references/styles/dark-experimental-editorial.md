# dark-experimental-editorial
Status: candidate knowledge; observed reference, not field validated.
## Visual grammar and execution
Tattoo site alternates moody cropped body photography with white textured grid sections, split oversized headings and narrow black chapter bars. Use chapter separators and alternating tonal density; ensure microcopy remains readable and booking controls accessible.
## Selection
Use when the brief benefits from this visual language; adapt palette, subject and typography to brand and audience rather than copying the reference.
