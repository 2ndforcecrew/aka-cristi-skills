# liquid-futurism
Status: candidate knowledge; observed reference, not field validated.
## Visual grammar and execution
Ice-blue translucent ribbons surround a low-angle athlete and oversized black background lettering; shoe sole repeats the blue accent. Use fluid curves to guide attention around rather than obscure the product; keep product silhouette accurate and text outside busy refractions.
## Selection
Use when the brief benefits from this visual language; adapt palette, subject and typography to brand and audience rather than copying the reference.
